Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-17.

This policy covers Fringe, the macOS app (including the Mac App Store version, "Fringe: Wave Optics"), and fringe-cli, its command-line tool. Both are made by Alper Saritas.

The short version

Fringe collects no data. Nothing you do in it leaves your Mac.

What Fringe does and does not do

  • No data collection. Fringe has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking and no accounts. The developer receives nothing about you or how you use the app.
  • No network access. The app and fringe-cli never connect to the internet. The Mac App Store version runs in Apple's App Sandbox without the permission to make network connections, so it cannot, even by mistake.
  • Your files stay where you put them. Fringe reads a file only when you open it, and writes a file only where you choose to save or export it (scenes, images, animation frames and field data). fringe-cli reads and writes only the files and folders you name on its command line.
  • Settings stay on your Mac. The app remembers your current scene and display settings between launches in its own macOS preferences, on your Mac (for the Mac App Store version, inside its sandbox container, ~/Library/Containers/com.asympton.fringe). They are never sent anywhere.

Apple

If you download Fringe from the Mac App Store, Apple handles the download, and may collect information under its own privacy policy. The developer can see only the aggregate figures Apple provides to all developers, such as download counts, and crash reports only if you choose to share them with developers in macOS settings.

Children

Fringe is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Its history is in this repository.
